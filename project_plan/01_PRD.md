# DOCUMENT 01 — PRD (Product Requirements Document)

**App Name & Tagline**
**AstraChat:** Your Universe, Unlocked.

**Problem**
People seek meaning, direction, and connection in their daily lives, but find traditional astrology too generic (newspaper horoscopes) or too complex to interpret (raw birth charts). They lack a personalized, interactive guide that can explain *why* they feel a certain way today based on real time transits and their unique natal placements.

**Target User**
Sarah, 26, is a creative professional who is spiritually curious and regularly checks astrology memes on Instagram. She wants deeper insights into her relationships and career timing, but finds human astrologers too expensive and traditional birth chart calculators visually overwhelming and confusing.

**Core Features — Must Have (v1)**
1. **User Profile & Birth Data Engine:** Precise collection of birth date, time, and city, with automatic timezone and coordinate resolution.
2. **Dynamic Birth Chart Generation:** A beautiful, mathematically accurate rendering of the user's natal wheel (houses, planets, degrees).
3. **Personalized Daily Horoscopes:** Three-part daily readings (Mood, Love, Career) customized specifically to the user's Sun, Moon, and Rising signs.
4. **Synastry Compatibility Engine:** The ability to add a partner's birth details to generate a 0-100 compatibility score and relationship breakdown.
5. **AI Astrologer Chat (Astra):** A context-aware chat interface powered by an LLM that knows the user's exact birth chart and can answer specific life questions.
6. **Cosmic Glossary:** Tap-to-reveal explanations for any astrological term (e.g., "What is a Trine?", "What does Moon in Scorpio mean?").
7. **Daily Push Notifications:** Personalized morning alerts hinting at the day's primary astrological transit affecting the user.

**Nice to Have — v2 features**
1. Daily Tarot Card pull (single card integration).
2. Live Astrologer consultations (booking & video capability).
3. Biorhythm & mood tracking calendar.
4. Transit timeline mapping (predictive lookahead for the month).
5. Social sharing integration (export visually stunning chart cards to Instagram Stories).

**Out of Scope for v1**
- E-commerce / Physical merchandise shop.
- Vedic/Sidereal astrology support (strictly Tropical/Western for v1).
- Multi-person group charts (only 1-on-1 synastry).

**User Stories**
1. As a new user, I want to enter my exact birth time and location so that the app can generate a mathematically accurate natal chart.
2. As a daily user, I want to read my personalized horoscope every morning so that I can prepare for the day's energetic themes.
3. As a curious user, I want to input my new date's birth details so that I can see our romantic compatibility score.
4. As a confused user, I want to ask the AI astrologer why I am feeling so anxious today so that I get answers based on my current transits.
5. As an astrology beginner, I want to tap on a planet in my chart so that I can read a simple explanation of what it means in my specific house.
6. As a returning user, I want to receive an engaging push notification at 8 AM so that I remember to check my daily insights.
7. As an engaged user, I want to view my chat history with the AI so that I can reflect on past advice.
8. As a privacy-conscious user, I want to easily delete my profile and birth data so that I feel secure.

**Success Metrics**
- D1 Retention > 40%, D7 Retention > 20%.
- Average session length > 3.5 minutes.
- > 5 AI chat messages sent per active user, per week.
- < 2% error rate in geocoding/chart generation.
