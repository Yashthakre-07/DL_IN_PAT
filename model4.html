Group Leader

Dr. Abhishek Subramanian

Assistant Professor, Department of Biotechnology, IIT Hyderabad (Dec 2022 – present). DBT–Ramalingaswami Re-entry Fellow. Computational biologist with ~12 years of experience spanning biological scales.

Formerly at CSIR-National Chemical Laboratory, Pune (PhD, Dr. Ram Rup Sarkar) and postdoctoral research at Prof. Peter Carmeliet's lab, VIB-KU Leuven Center for Cancer Biology, Belgium — one of the world's most renowned vascular biology labs.

Expertise spans constraint-based modeling, kinetic modeling, omics data integration, statistical regression, machine learning, genomics, transcriptomics, and bioinformatics. Specifically interested in metabolic adaptations, interspecies interactions during infection, and immunometabolism.

What We Do

Research Areas

Our group asks questions that facilitate understanding of design principles of biological systems, using computational techniques to discover new molecular/pathway mechanisms from raw biological data.

Computational Systems & Network Biology

Constraint-based genome-scale modeling, graph theory, and condition-specific statistical inference of biological networks — metabolism, gene regulation, and protein-protein interaction networks.

→ GEMs · FBA · Graph Theory

Omics Data Analysis & Bioinformatics

Integration of transcriptomics, proteomics, single-cell RNA-seq, and metabolomics data into biological networks. Big Data Biology: from omics to function — uncovering pathway mechanisms from raw biological data.

→ RNA-Seq · scRNA-seq · Multi-Omics

Metabolism & Gene Regulation

Metabolic adaptations in parasites, endothelial cells, and pathogens. Host-parasite metabolic interactions. Condition-specific metabolic reprogramming in infection and disease. Angiogenesis metabolism.

→ Leishmania · Endothelial Cells · Immunometabolism

Mathematical, Statistical Modelling & ML

Machine learning pipelines, kinetic modeling of biochemical pathways, population dynamics, and statistical regression models. Discovering essential enzymes in metabolic networks using ML approaches.

→ Machine Learning · Kinetic Models · Regression

Parasitology & Immunology

Host-parasite metabolic interactions in Cryptosporidium parvum, Cryptococcus gattii, and Aspergillus fumigatus infections. Parasite-dependent macrophage transition. Immunometabolism in disease contexts.

→ Infection Biology · Macrophage Biology · Leishmaniasis

Tool & Database Development

NAViFluX — a visualization-centric platform for interactive analysis of genome-scale metabolic networks. BIOMEX for single-cell omics data interpretation. Open-source pipelines for the community.

→ NAViFluX · BIOMEX · Open Source

Software

Tools & Databases

Open-source platforms and pipelines developed by our lab for systems biology and multi-omics analysis.

NAViFluX

A visualization-centric, web-based platform for interactive analysis, refinement, and engineering of genome-scale metabolic networks (GSMNs). It integrates pathway visualization, flux balance analysis, enrichment analysis, and network topology exploration. Unlike conventional tools, NAViFluX enables direct manipulation of reactions and real-time flux observation.

💻 GitHub 📄 Paper

BIOMEX

An interactive workflow for (single cell) omics data interpretation and visualization. BIOMEX provides a comprehensive environment for processing and analyzing multi-omics datasets, facilitating biological insight discovery from complex multi-dimensional data.
